# Code of Conduct

Atlas Research is a technical community where evidence can be challenged and
people must be treated with care.

## Working together

- Critique an idea, patch, or measurement without attacking its author.
- State assumptions, uncertainty, and conflicts of interest.
- Make room for questions and different levels of experience.
- Respect names, identities, boundaries, and private information.
- Correct mistakes openly and help leave the project easier to understand.

Harassment, discriminatory abuse, threats, sexualized attention, doxxing,
deliberate intimidation, and repeated disruption are not accepted. Publishing a
person's private information or confidential report without permission is also
prohibited.

## Where this applies

The rules cover this repository, its issue and review spaces, project events,
and public interactions made on behalf of Atlas Research.

Maintainers may hide or remove harmful content, stop an interaction, reject a
contribution, or restrict participation. Responses should be proportional,
documented where privacy permits, and free from retaliation.

## Reporting

Do not place sensitive details in a public issue. Open a minimal issue titled
`Code of Conduct contact request` and ask a maintainer to establish a private
channel. A maintainer named in the report must not adjudicate it alone.

The current maintainers interpret and enforce this policy. The project does not
promise a response-time SLA during its pre-release phase.
